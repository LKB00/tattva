Shop assistant: slow orders

A shop owner asks why orders slowed down last week.

The problem

The answer is one long block of text. It does not say where the facts came from, and it sounds equally sure about everything.

First version

Why did orders slow down last week?

Orders slowed down last week mainly because of a rise in shipping delays at your main carrier, combined with a change in how many people visited your shop on weekends. There was also a small increase in the number of people who left items in their cart, which usually means checkout felt harder than before. Your new product page went live on Tuesday, and customers may have found it confusing. In addition, a sale at a similar shop could have pulled shoppers away. Most shops see this kind of dip now and then, so it is probably nothing to worry about, but you might want to look at your shipping settings and your checkout page to be safe.

Improved

Why did orders slow down last week?
I compared daily orders to the week before, then looked at where shoppers stopped.

Output

2 reports read

Orders dropped after Tuesday, the day the new product page went live1. More shoppers also left on the payment step than the week before2.

The timing points to the new page, but the reports cannot prove it. A weekend dip would look similar.

Sources

  1. 1Orders per day, last 14 daysYour shop reportsDaily orders fell from Tuesday and stayed lower.
  2. 2Checkout visits and exitsYour shop reportsMore people left on the payment step.
Medium confidenceModerate confidence. Spot-check key facts.

What changed

  1. 1

    Show the work

    A short note and a list of steps the assistant took let the owner see what was checked.

    • ThinkingBlock
    • ToolCallCard
  2. 2

    Point to sources

    Small numbers in the text link to the reports behind each claim.

    • CitationMarker
    • SourceList
  3. 3

    Say how sure it is

    A confidence label tells the owner how much to lean on the answer.

    • ConfidenceIndicator
  4. 4

    Mark it as AI

    A small label says the answer was written by an assistant.

    • AILabel
    • Message

The result

The owner can see what was checked, click through to the reports, and tell which parts are solid and which are a guess.
