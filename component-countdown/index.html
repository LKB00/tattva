Countdown

Stable

A deadline as a large date, the days left in words, and an optional bar of time used.

Also known as timer, deadline, time remaining

Install with npm install @tattva/ui (setup), or copy the code from the kit in Downloads.

With a time bar

They must reply by

27 October 2026

20 days left

<Countdown className="w-full max-w-sm" deadline="2026-10-27" today="2026-10-07" start="2026-09-27" label="They must reply by" />

A start date adds the bar. The bar has the words 10 of 30 days used for screen readers.

UsageCodeDesignAccessibility

When to use it

Answers the first question about a case: by when. The date is the largest text and the days left are written out, so nothing depends on colour or a bar.

Made of: Label · Action chip · Date · Days in words · Time bar

Use it for

  • A reply-by or pay-by date at the top of a case.
  • A deadline with a start date, to show how much time has gone.
  • A missed deadline that now needs a person to take a step.

Not for

  • A plain number with how it changed: use StatTile.
  • How much of an allowance is used: use Meter.
  • A status label such as Overdue in a row: use Badge.

Do and avoid

Do

  • Put it near the top, since the date is the first thing people look for.
  • Pass start when the person needs to see how much time has gone.
  • Use needsAction only when the next step belongs to a person.

Avoid

  • Do not use amber because a date is close. Use it when a person must act.
  • Do not rely on the bar. The days are always written in words.
  • Do not use it for dates measured in hours or minutes.

On a phone

  • It is a card that fills its container, with the date in large text that breaks anywhere if it is too long.
  • The days left are in words and the bar is a picture of the same thing, so a small screen loses nothing.
  • Nothing in it is tapped or hovered.

Works with

  • StatTile
  • Meter
  • Badge
  • Callout

States

States this part has beyond its default look. Build each one your screen can reach. States set by a property are in the Code tab.

days left
The default. The date is large and the days left are written in words.
due today
When the deadline is today the line reads Due today.
missed
After the deadline the missed label shows with an icon, followed by the days past.
needs action
With needsAction, an amber chip shows. If the deadline is today or past, the border and bar turn amber too.
no date
If the deadline cannot be read as a date, an em dash shows in place of the date and the days line is left out.

Tokens

Built in

  • The card is a group named by the label.
  • The date is a time element with the ISO date.
  • Days left, due today and days past are written in words, so colour is never the only cue.
  • A missed deadline shows an icon and the missed label.
  • The time bar is a meter with the text N of M days used.
  • Dates are worked out from the calendar day only, so time zones and daylight saving cannot shift the count.

What you need to do

  1. 1Give a label that says whose date it is, such as They must reply by.
  2. 2Pass needsAction only when a person has to act. Amber is not decoration.
  3. 3Pass today in docs and tests so the days left do not change under you.

Built toward WCAG 2.2 AA and checked automatically; no independent audit yet. Read the accessibility guide
