Components/Create

Create

Images, voice, video, slides and music. 49 parts.

49 parts

Creating14, 14 parts

  • PromptBoxA box for describing what you want made, with a Generate button and optional examples.
  • ParameterPanelA set of choices for making something with AI, with a live cost and a Generate button.
  • VariationActionsA row of things you can do with one AI result, each with a Subtle or Strong choice, and a line saying where it came from.
  • VariantGridA set of made options where you pick one, with empty slots held for ones still coming.
  • VariantTileA square tile for one AI-made option that a person can pick.
  • VersionHistoryA list of earlier versions of your work, with who made each, when, and Preview and Restore buttons.
  • GenerationProgressExperimentalShows how a slow AI task is going: waiting, making it, ready to listen early, finishing, done, failed or cancelled.
  • CanvasPanelA side panel for a document, code or design that stays after the chat message.
  • SuggestionBarA bar under an AI draft. Nothing changes in your document until you choose.
  • GhostTextFaded suggested text that appears after what you typed, with a Tab key hint.
  • SelectionToolbarA small bar that appears over highlighted text, with quick edits, a menu of more, and a box to ask for your own.
  • SuggestionCardOne writing suggestion with its type, the before and after, a reason, and Accept and Dismiss buttons.
  • MediaFrameAn image in a fixed shape, with a placeholder while it loads, a message if it fails, and an optional caption and AI label.
  • MediaGridExperimentalA gallery of images in even rows that adjusts to the screen, with optional selecting and an empty message.

Voice and audio16, 16 parts

  • VoicePanelA voice conversation (VoiceSession) on the shared AI disc, with every mic, turn and connection state in words, captions, and separate Mute, Hold and End.
  • MicButtonThe one mic control for dictation and starting voice, with every mic state written next to it: recording, locked, turning speech into text, blocked and no mic.
  • LiveCaptionsA streaming caption log for voice that marks words still changing, words the assistant never said, and hidden numbers.
  • AudioPlayerPlays one clip: play and pause, a waveform to seek, time, speed and download.
  • WaveformThe loudness of a clip drawn as bars, with the played part filled. Can also be the seek control.
  • TranscriptSyncA recording's transcript that follows playback. Press a line to play from there.
  • VoicePickerA list of voices to hear and choose from, with tag filters. Only one sample plays at a time.
  • VoiceCardOne voice to hear and choose: name, how it sounds, who owns it, whether you can use it, and a sample button.
  • VoiceConsentCheckA live check that the person whose voice or likeness is being copied is present and agrees, by reading prompted lines aloud.
  • CallRecordOne call at a glance for the people who run a voice agent: status, why it ended, the AI's verdict with evidence, the cost and anything a person must fix.
  • LatencyBreakdownShows where the time went in a voice reply or a whole call, names the slowest stage in words and lists the turns that were too slow.
  • TakePickerCompare a few AI-made takes of the same request, hear them at the same moment, and keep one.
  • SectionLaneA song's structure as labelled sections on a timeline: select, regenerate one, extend after it or move it, with staged edits and out-of-date sections in amber words.
  • StemMixerOne lane per stem of a song with Mute, Solo, a named level slider and a box to pick it for export, plus the separation state in words.
  • TranscriptAudioEditorEdit recorded speech by editing its words: remove words to cut the audio, or change words to make new audio in a consented voice.
  • EnhanceCompareClean up a recording with one strength slider, and switch Before and After at the same moment.

Video10, 10 parts

  • VideoPlayerPlays a generated clip and lets you act on the exact frame you paused on.
  • FrameScrubberThe seek slider for a video clip: time in words, frame steps and a preview while dragging.
  • ClipRangeSelectorPick the start and end of the part of a clip to extend, edit or re-do, with the longest length built in.
  • TakeCardOne generated video take, from the queue to the finished clip, with a charge line that says whether credits were taken.
  • TakeGridA set of video takes where running and finished takes sit together, with one combined announcement, keyboard moves, and empty and loading views.
  • TakeVersionsThe versions of one video take, each saying what it came from: extend, edit, remix, re-cut or upscale, with preview, make current and undo.
  • ShotSettingsSettings for one video generation, with the cost shown as takes × cost per take before Generate is pressed.
  • CameraMovePickerPick a camera move such as push in, pan or orbit by name, with a small diagram for each.
  • SceneStripAn ordered storyboard of shots with a status on each, a total length, and reorder by drag, buttons or keyboard.
  • LikenessConsentAsk for, track and control a real person's consent to use their face or voice in avatars.

Slides9, 9 parts

  • OutlineEditorThe plan of a deck before any slide is built: the AI drafts it, the person edits, reorders and approves it.
  • SlideRailThe list of slide thumbnails: move around, choose slides, reorder, and see each slide's state in words.
  • SlideStageThe large view of one slide at its true shape, scaled to fit, with its state and things to check.
  • LayoutAlternativesOther layouts for one slide, suggested by the AI and compared with the slide as it is. Preview without changing the deck, then apply one or keep the current.
  • DeckChangeReviewReview an AI edit that changed several slides: before and after for each slide, Keep or Undo each one or all at once, then save the kept changes as one version.
  • DeckBuildProgressProgress for a deck the AI builds slide by slide: slides ready out of the total, the real step, Stop that keeps what is done, and Retry per failed slide.
  • SpeakerNotesNotes for one slide: an AI draft held apart until you keep it, "AI draft" or "Edited by you", and the word count and speaking time worked out in code.
  • ExportSheetExport a deck as PDF, PowerPoint, images or a link: what the file holds, what needs fixing first (amber) and what will change, then progress, the file name, or Retry.
  • EditScopeChipSays exactly what an AI edit will change, such as Slide 3 or Whole deck, and lets the person change it before sending.
