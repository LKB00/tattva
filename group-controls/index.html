Controls

11 parts in this group. Pick one to open it.

11 parts

  • ButtonUpdatedA rounded button with a text label. It comes in five styles and three sizes, and can hold an icon.
  • IconButtonA round button that shows only an icon. It always has a name, and it can show an on or off state.
  • BadgeUpdatedA small label that shows a status, a count or a category.
  • AvatarA round picture that shows who wrote a message: a letter for a person, a dark circle with a lime spark for the assistant.
  • AvatarStackNewPro, locked. The preview is free, the code comes with Pro.A few overlapping avatars, a plus, then how many more people there are.
  • SegmentedControlUpdatedA row of joined buttons where you pick one option, using clicks or arrow keys.
  • KeyHintA small key label, such as Tab or Esc, that shows which key to press.
  • PopoverUpdatedA small panel that opens beside a button, for menus, pickers and short explanations.
  • CollapsibleUpdatedA heading with an arrow that shows or hides extra content when pressed.
  • SectionLabelA tiny capital-letter label that names a group in a list, with room for a small action.
  • AttentionDotA small amber dot that means a person has to do something.
