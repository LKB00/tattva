Wraps on wide screens, swipes on a phone.

UsageCodeDesignAccessibility

When to use it

Lets people compare a handful of results at a glance, on any screen.

Made of: Row · Card

Use it for

  • Two to eight results of the same kind.
  • Results in a chat answer that must fit a phone.

Not for

  • Many results people filter and sort: use DataTable.
  • Pictures the AI made, to pick from: use VariantGrid.

Do and avoid

Do

  • Use it for results of the same kind.
  • Keep to a handful; link to a full list for more.

Avoid

  • Do not mix kinds of results in one row.
  • Do not hide the scroll on a phone; the peeking card shows there is more.

On a phone

  • Cards swipe sideways and snap into place, each at most 85% of the width.
  • The row can be scrolled with the keyboard once it has focus.

Seen in products

  • OpenUI lays out repeated card blocks with a Carousel, and every item in the block has the same structure.
  • OpenUI, openuiLibrary.tsx (card blocks, Carousel, EntityList) (opens in a new tab)

Works with

  • ResultCard
  • VariantGrid

States

States this part has beyond its default look. Build each one your screen can reach. States set by a property are in the Code tab.

wide
A grid that wraps; cards in a row share one height.
phone
A row that swipes sideways and snaps, with the next card peeking in.
focus
The row shows a focus ring and scrolls with the arrow keys on a phone.

Tokens

Change one of these and every part that uses it follows.

  • --spacing
  • --focus-ring

Built in

  • A list with a label, so its length and name are announced.
  • On a phone the row takes keyboard focus and scrolls with the arrow keys.

What you need to do

  1. 1Give the row a label that says what is in it, such as Hotels in Paris.
  2. 2Keep the cards in the order the answer ranks them.
