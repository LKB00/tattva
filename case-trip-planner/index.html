Trip planner

A traveler asks for a three day plan for a city.

The problem

One long answer arrives. If the traveler wants a calmer pace or a different focus, they have to start over.

First version

Plan a 3 day trip to Lisbon.

Day 1: Arrive, walk through Alfama, visit the castle, eat dinner near the river. Day 2: Take the tram to Belem, try the custard tarts, visit the monastery, then spend the evening in Bairro Alto. Day 3: Take a day trip to Sintra to see the palaces, return in the late afternoon, and finish with a quiet dinner. Have a great trip!

Improved

Tune the plan

Lisbon, 3 days

Food focus, balanced pace

  1. Day 1. Morning market and coffee

What changed

  1. 1

    Offer a few directions

    Several plans appear side by side, and the traveler picks the one that feels right.

    • VariantGrid
  2. 2

    Adjust with controls

    Pace and length are simple choices, not something to retype.

    • ParameterPanel
  3. 3

    Keep every version

    Each new plan is saved beside the plan on screen, so earlier ones are one click away.

    • CanvasPanel

The result

The traveler shapes the plan step by step and can go back to an earlier version without losing anything.
