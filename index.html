Questions

Do I need to know React?

No. Designers can work in Figma with the plugin, and anyone can turn a few answers into one prompt for an AI tool. To ship the components in your own product, your app needs React. Pick your path.

What does an AI tool get from Tattva?

Each component, when to use it, its rules and its examples. Connect Claude Code, Cursor or Copilot through the MCP server, or point it at llms.txt and components.json. Connect a tool.

Can I use my brand colours?

Yes. Seven themes come ready, each in light and dark, and every colour is a design token you can change in one place. How colour works.

Is it really free?

Yes. All 223 components, the docs, the design tokens, the Figma plugin and the AI tools kit are free, with the full code. See the downloads.

How is accessibility checked?

Every release runs axe on every page in every theme, light and dark, plus a keyboard walk, phone widths and real clicks. There has been no independent audit yet. Read the guide.
