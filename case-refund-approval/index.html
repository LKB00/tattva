Shop assistant: refunds

A shop owner lets an assistant refund customers for late orders.

The problem

The assistant asks a bare Yes or No question. The owner cannot tell who gets money, how much, or what saying yes allows later.

First version

Do you want to continue?

The assistant would like to issue refunds.

Improved

Refund 3 late orders

Awaiting approval
Nothing has changed yetTakes about a minute
  1. 1

    Find late orders

    Orders that arrived more than 5 days after the promised date.

  2. 2

    Refund the shipping fee

    Only the shipping fee on each order. Item prices stay as paid.

  3. 3

    Email each customer

    A short note saying the refund is on its way.

What changed

  1. 1

    Show the plan first

    The steps are listed in plain words, with a time estimate, before anything happens.

    • PlanCard
  2. 2

    Spell out the exact action

    The request lists each customer and amount, and why a person has to decide.

    • PermissionPrompt
  3. 3

    Limit what a yes covers

    The owner can allow once, or save a narrow rule that lists exactly what it covers.

    • PermissionPrompt
  4. 4

    Make Deny useful

    Deny takes a short note, so the assistant learns what to do instead.

    • PermissionPrompt

The result

The owner knows exactly what will happen before agreeing, can approve a small piece, and can say no and explain why.
