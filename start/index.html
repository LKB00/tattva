Docs/Start/Introduction

Start here

Tell us what you are doing. You only see the steps that matter to you, and nothing needs installing to look around.

I am

Your AI tool does the building. You answer a few questions, copy one prompt, and paste it. No code and no install.

  1. 1

    Make your prompt

    Answer five plain questions on the Build brief page. It writes the prompt for you.
  2. 2

    Paste it into your AI tool

    Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Lovable, v0 or Bolt. It knows which parts to use and the rules that keep the product trustworthy.
  3. 3

    Want better results? Connect it (optional)

    Connecting lets the tool look things up itself and check its own work. Connect an AI tool shows how, step by step.
Make my prompt

Plain words

Part (component)
One ready-made piece of interface, like a message box or an approval card.
Pattern
A whole moment built from parts, like "ask before the AI does something risky".
Theme
A set of colours. Tattva has 7. Pick one with the Theme button at the top.
Token
A named colour, size or timing, such as "surface" or "text muted". Change it once and everything follows.
AI tool
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Lovable, v0, Bolt and similar. They write the code when you tell them what you want.
Prompt
The message you give an AI tool. The Build brief page writes a good one for you.
MCP
A small helper that lets an AI tool look things up in Tattva by itself and check its own work. Optional.
llms.txt
A plain index of the whole system, written for AI tools to read. You never need to open it.
