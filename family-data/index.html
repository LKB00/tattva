Components/Data

Data

Charts, tables, markets and screens the assistant builds. 30 parts.

30 parts

Numbers16, 16 parts

  • ChartFrameThe box every chart sits in. It adds a title, a short written summary, a table view and clear messages when data is loading or missing.
  • DataTableThe numbers behind a chart, in a plain table with digits lined up.
  • StatTileOne big number with how it changed, an optional tiny trend line and an optional limit.
  • SparklineA tiny trend line that highlights the latest value and can be read aloud.
  • LineChartA line chart for change over time. Lines are named at their ends and can be explored with the keyboard.
  • BarChartBars that compare amounts. Highlight one bar, or stack up to four parts.
  • DotPlotShows a best guess and how sure it is, with a caption that explains the marks.
  • ChartLegendA key that explains each color, dash and marker in a chart, in the chart's order. It can also switch lines on and off.
  • UsageMeterHow many credits are left, with an optional cost estimate, reset date and upgrade button.
  • ContextMeterExperimentalShows how full the chat is, with a Summarize button when it is nearly full.
  • MeterBarA thin bar that shows how much of something is used, and turns amber past a set point.
  • TimelineExperimentalA top-to-bottom list of events with times and an optional status.
  • ComparisonTableExperimentalCompares 2 to 5 things side by side. On a small screen, it becomes stacked cards.
  • ProcessDiagramA flow chart of steps and arrows, with a written version for people who cannot see it.
  • ChartRangeBrushA strip under a long chart with two handles. The part between them is what the chart above shows.
  • DiagramFigureWraps a diagram with a caption, a full description and a way to view its source.

Markets and trading11, 11 parts

  • PriceA live price that tints briefly and shows an arrow for its last change.
  • PriceChangeA signed change with an arrow, as an amount, a percent or both.
  • DataFreshnessSays how fresh the numbers are, in words and an icon.
  • TickerTapeA looping strip of symbols, prices and changes, with a visible Pause button.
  • CandlestickChartPrice candles for open, high, low and close. Up and down are told apart by shape and by words, not by colour alone.
  • HeatmapA grid of coloured cells with the number printed in each one. Use it to spot patterns across two things.
  • PayoffChartShows the profit or loss at the end for a position as the price moves, with break-even prices and best and worst outcomes labelled.
  • OrderTicketA buy or sell form to place a trade: side, quantity, order type, price, product, a review summary and one submit button.
  • OptionChainCalls on the left, puts on the right, strike in the middle, as a real table.
  • ActionDraftThe draft an AI proposes, edited in place, confirmed by the person, then shown as progress.
  • RiskLimitsThe limits that protect the person, with a kill switch that asks first, in place.

Generated interfaces3, 3 parts

  • GenUIRendererDraws a screen an assistant wrote, using only approved parts, and sends what the person does back.
  • GeneratedSurfaceExperimentalThe frame around a screen an assistant made: who made it, its spec, pin, redo and report.
  • SandboxedFrameA locked box for a mini app made by someone else, with clear limits and asks before it does anything.
