Components/Chat

Chat

Messages, the composer, thinking, and assistants that do work. 56 parts.

56 parts

Conversation33, 33 parts

  • MessageOne turn in a conversation: a bubble for the person, open text across the full width for the assistant.
  • MessageListA column that holds a conversation and reads out new messages.
  • ComposerThe box where people type a message, with attach, tools and send or stop.
  • EditableTurnA message the person sent, which they can edit and send again, with its earlier versions kept.
  • ContinueNoticeSays why a reply ended early and offers to carry on.
  • FileResultCardA file the AI made, with what kind it is, a preview, Open and Download.
  • FileDropZoneWhere people drop or browse for files, with the kinds and sizes that work said up front.
  • SharePanelWho can open a chat or a result, with its link to copy and a way to stop sharing.
  • ProjectPanelA project's instructions and files, shared by every chat in it.
  • ResearchRunA long research run: its phases, how many sources it has read, Stop, then the report.
  • ConversationListPast chats grouped by day, with a New chat button, for the side bar.
  • MarkdownTurns the assistant's formatted text into headings, lists, links and tables, even while it is still being written.
  • SuggestionChipsA row of ready-made questions a person can pick.
  • MessageActionsCopy and retry buttons that sit under an assistant reply.
  • ThinkingBlockA closed note of the assistant's thinking. It shimmers while it works and shows how long it took when done.
  • TypingIndicatorThree pulsing dots that show the assistant is getting ready to reply.
  • ShimmerTextStatus text with a moving shine, for steps like "Searching the web…".
  • HeroHeadingA big centered headline that types itself out once, then stays still.
  • ContextPillA small tag near the message box that shows what the chat is about.
  • ModelPickerA small button that opens a list for choosing how the assistant answers, with optional thinking time and limit messages.
  • ToolChipA small tag in the message box showing a tool that is on, with a Remove button.
  • ToolMenuThe plus menu by the message box, with ways to attach things, tools you can switch on, and connected apps.
  • AttachmentChipA tag for an attached file or image, with name, size, upload progress, an error with Retry, and Remove.
  • MessageQueueMessages sent while the assistant is busy, with Send now, reorder and delete.
  • VersionPagerPrevious and next buttons with a count like 1/3, for the versions of one message.
  • JumpToLatestA small floating button that takes you back to the newest message.
  • StreamingRevealText that appears as it is written, with each new word fading in once.
  • StarterGalleryA first screen that says what the assistant can do, and offers ideas to start with.
  • CodeBlockA dark box for code, with the language name and a Copy button.
  • TrayA soft panel with a short list of things to look at, and an optional message box under it.
  • TrayRowA compact list row with an icon, a title, a detail line and a hint that shows on hover.
  • AIPresenceA moving orb that shows whether the assistant is waiting, listening, thinking, speaking, or has hit a problem.
  • ResponseCompareExperimentalTwo replies side by side, and the person picks the better one.

Assistants23, 23 parts

  • PlanCardShows what the assistant plans to do before it starts, so you can approve or ask for changes.
  • StepTimelineA list of steps in a task, showing which are done, in progress, waiting or failed.
  • ChecklistA list of what is still open, with finished work folded into one line.
  • ChecklistRowA list row with a title, an optional tag and a status on the right. An amber dot marks items that need a person.
  • ToolCallCardA card that shows one step the assistant took, with its status, how long it took, and what went in and out.
  • TaskTreeA list of tasks and the smaller tasks inside them, each with a status and an Open button.
  • AgentSessionListA list of assistant tasks grouped by what they need, with a Peek panel to read the latest and reply.
  • AgentStatusIconA small icon that shows what an assistant is doing right now, with a different shape for each state.
  • PermissionPromptAsks you to allow one thing the assistant wants to do, with a choice to always allow it and an optional note.
  • ApprovalPromptA pause that asks a person before the assistant does something important.
  • PermissionModeSwitcherA button that always shows how much freedom the assistant has, and lets you change it.
  • DiffViewReview the changes an assistant made to your files, and keep or reject each one.
  • DiffFileRowOne changed file in a review, with how much was added and removed, and buttons to keep or reject it.
  • CheckpointListA list of earlier steps you can go back to, with restore and summarize buttons and a confirm step.
  • TakeoverBarA full-width bar that says who is in control: you, or the assistant.
  • AgentQuestionCardThe assistant asks you a question in the middle of a task, with quick answers.
  • NeedsYouInboxOne list of everything across your runs that is waiting for you.
  • BackgroundRunChipA small chip for a run in the background that opens its details.
  • ReEntryRecapA "While you were away" summary shown when you come back to a run.
  • ActionLogA lasting list of what an assistant did, with who approved it and a timed Undo.
  • ApprovedByMarkExperimentalA small mark that says who said yes to an action.
  • BudgetControlShows and sets how much time, money and steps a run may use, and what happens at the limit.
  • AltitudeToggleSwitch between a short summary, each step, or every detail of what an assistant did.
