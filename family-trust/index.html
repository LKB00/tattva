Components/Trust

Trust

Sources, confidence, asking first, and showing the work. 37 parts.

37 parts

Trust22, 22 parts

  • ClaimCheckA reply with the sentences no source backs up marked in place.
  • SensitiveDataGuardWarns before a message with a card number, password or other private detail is sent.
  • ConnectorPermissionOne connected app, and exactly what the AI can do with it.
  • ContextPanelExperimentalEverything the AI can see right now, each item removable.
  • AILabelA small lime tag that says content was made by AI and explains how. Once a person edits the content, it becomes an undo button.
  • AIMarkA small four-point shape that tells people something was made by AI.
  • AIBadgeA lime-tinted badge with a sparkle that marks content made by AI.
  • AIDisclosureA small centered note reminding people to check the assistant's answers.
  • ProvenancePopoverA label that says content was made with AI, and opens a short note on how.
  • CitationMarkerA small numbered link that points to a source, placed after a claim in a reply.
  • CitationHoverCardA small number in a sentence that shows where a fact came from.
  • SourceCardA numbered link card for one source.
  • SourceListThe numbered sources behind an answer, shown in two columns.
  • ConfidenceIndicatorThree bars and a label that show how sure the assistant is about an answer.
  • FeedbackBarThumbs up and down buttons, with a short list of reasons after a thumbs down.
  • RefusalNoticeA calm notice for a request the assistant will not do, with an optional alternative.
  • SummaryCardAn AI-written overview with its sources, a warning to double-check, and a way to give feedback.
  • RevertToggleLets people flip between the AI's suggestion and their own edit, with a way back to the original.
  • MemoryNoticeA short notice that says what was saved to memory, with Manage, Undo and close.
  • MemoryListA list of what the assistant remembers. Each item can be edited or deleted. Two switches sit on top.
  • PrivateChatBannerA notice that stays visible during a private chat, says how long a copy is kept, and has Exit.
  • KeyTakeawayExperimentalA labeled box for the closing point of an answer.

Asking and showing work15, 15 parts

  • QuestionSetOne card that asks every missing fact at once, one question at a time, with a single final button.
  • ActivityTraceThe agent's work shown step by step, each step tagged with who did it, then folded to one line.
  • ReceiptCollapse a card the person already answered into one line. It opens again to show the full card.
  • DraftPreviewA drafted message shown like an email, with headers and a body that opens up to the full text.
  • CountdownA deadline as a large date, the days left in words, and an optional bar of time used.
  • StatusLineOne quiet line of status for the top of a screen, such as Case, Flipkart, an amount and Waiting.
  • MoneyAn amount that code worked out, in mono with tabular figures, in rupees by default.
  • GlancePanelThe case at a glance: the deadline, the facts, the rule and the steps, kept in view beside a chat. GlanceSheet is the phone version.
  • ScrollEdgeA scrolling area whose top and bottom edges blur gradually, so content softens near the edge instead of meeting a hard line.
  • StatusTickerOne line that shows the real current step of an AI run, such as Reading 3 files, and keeps its width so nothing beside it moves.
  • WorkingEdgeA calm moving ring in the AI colour around a surface, shown only while the AI is working on it.
  • LiveLogA streaming, monospaced log of what an agent is doing, with pause and follow.
  • JsonViewShows a tool call's input or output as a tree you can open level by level, with a copy button.
  • InlineConfirmA button that asks "Delete this?" in its own place, with the safe choice focused, instead of opening a dialog.
  • PromptIndexA short list of the person's own messages in a long thread, so they can jump back to one.
