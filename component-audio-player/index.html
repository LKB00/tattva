Components/Create/AudioPlayer

AudioPlayer

Stable

Plays one clip: play and pause, a waveform to seek, time, speed and download.

Install with npm install @tattva/ui (setup), or copy the code from the kit in Downloads.

Call recording

Call with Maya Okafor

Support call, 3 October, 14:02

Tool call: look up order 4471 at 41 seconds. Caller interrupted the assistant at 1 minute 36 seconds. Needs review: Transfer to a person, check the reason at 2 minutes 38 seconds.
0:00 / 3:12
<AudioPlayer
  title="Call with Maya Okafor"
  subtitle="Support call, 3 October, 14:02"
  duration={192}
  peaks={peaks}
  markers={[
    { at: 41, label: "Tool call: look up order 4471" },
    { at: 96, label: "Caller interrupted the assistant" },
    { at: 158, label: "Transfer to a person, check the reason", tone: "review" },
  ]}
  onDownload={download}
/>

// With a real file, the length comes from the file:
<AudioPlayer title="Call with Maya Okafor" src="/recordings/4471.mp3" peaks={peaks} />

No file is needed for a preview: the player simulates playback from duration. Press Play, then try the arrow keys on the waveform, or Space and K anywhere in the player.

UsageCodeDesignAccessibility

When to use it

Lets someone listen back, jump to a moment and change the speed, with every state written in words.

Made of: Play button · Title · AI label · Subtitle or state · Waveform · Time · Speed · Download

Use it for

  • Listening back to a support or sales call.
  • Playing a voice or song the AI made, with an AI label.
  • A list of short takes, using the compact row.

Not for

  • A live conversation with the assistant: use VoicePanel.
  • A recording with a transcript that follows along: use TranscriptSync.
  • Video: use MediaFrame.

Do and avoid

Do

  • Wait for a press before playing anything.
  • Mark only the moments people need, such as tool calls, interruptions and the end.
  • Say why a clip cannot be played or downloaded, and what is still there.
  • Add the AI label to clips the AI made, and keep the rest of the player neutral.

Avoid

  • Don't autoplay, or play a preview on hover.
  • Don't colour the waveform or the Play button with the AI colour.
  • Don't let two players play at once. This one pauses the others for you.
  • Don't use amber for "recording" or "new take ready". Neither needs a person to act.

On a phone

  • The Play button is 44px. Speed and Download grow to 44px tall on touch screens.
  • The seek area is at least 44px tall. Tap to seek, and a time bubble shows while dragging.
  • The bottom row wraps when space is short. On the narrowest phones the waveform becomes a plain bar.
  • While playing it sets the lock-screen title and play, pause and seek controls where the browser supports them.
  • The compact row is 56px tall.

Seen in products

  • Vapi call logs have a waveform you can play and scrub, with downloads for mono, stereo or each side.
  • Alexa voice history lets you play each recording.
  • NotebookLM audio overviews offer playback speed, download, feedback and an AI label.
  • Suno limits downloads per month and shows how many are left.
  • Vapi: Call logs (opens in a new tab)
  • Music Business Worldwide: Suno download limits (opens in a new tab)
  • WCAG 2.2: Understanding Audio Control (opens in a new tab)

Built in

  • The player is a region named by its title.
  • Play is a toggle button named Play with aria-pressed, true while playing. When it cannot play it uses aria-disabled and stays focusable.
  • Space and K play or pause while focus is in the player, but not in the speed choice or any text field, and Space on a button keeps its usual meaning.
  • The waveform is a slider with the time in words, such as "52 seconds of 3 minutes 12 seconds"; see Waveform for its keys.
  • Buffering, ended, streaming, error and unavailable are announced once through a status region. The clock is never announced.
  • The time uses tabular figures, so it does not jitter.
  • Speed is a native select named Playback speed.
  • Under reduced motion the simulated clock ticks four times a second, so the playhead moves in steps.

What you need to do

  1. 1Give a title that tells clips apart, such as "Call with Maya Okafor". It names the player.
  2. 2Never start playback without a press. Autoplay talks over screen readers.
  3. 3Offer a transcript for recorded speech, with TranscriptSync or a link.
  4. 4Pass the error and unavailable reasons in plain words, and onRetry when trying again can help.

Built toward WCAG 2.2 AA and checked automatically; no independent audit yet. Read the accessibility guide
